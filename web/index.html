Detailed evaluation · numbers & methods

Message-level scores describe this input. Conversation-level values include earlier turns with the same conversation ID.

Across this conversation

Remaining privacy capacity

Higher means less accumulated exposure across four attributes. Range 0–4; sum of (1 − cumulative attribute risk). Technical name: Residual Anonymity Capacity. This is a heuristic index, not entropy in bits.

Added risk from this message

Increase in the highest cumulative attribute risk since the previous turn. The first turn is compared with zero. Zero does not mean no private information. Technical name: Leakage Delta.

How much meaning was preserved?

Text similarity

Cosine Similarity: higher means more similarity. Similar wording alone does not guarantee key details are preserved.

Support for rewritten content

Forward NLI: whether the original supports the rewrite. Higher suggests fewer unsupported additions.

Retention of original details

Backward NLI: whether the rewrite supports the original. Higher suggests fewer details were lost.

Overall meaning-preservation score

Composite Utility: combines similarity and both NLI directions, penalizing contradiction and degenerate output. Higher is better; it does not measure privacy improvement.
